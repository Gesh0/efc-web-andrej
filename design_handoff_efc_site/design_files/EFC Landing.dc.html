How it Works

The journey of every product.

From the moment a shipment arrives at our dock to the moment it ships — or comes back — every step is handled, tracked, and accounted for.

01

Arrival & Acceptance

Incoming shipments are received at the dock, logged, and checked against your inbound manifest.

02

Counting & Verification

Every unit is counted and verified for quantity, condition, and SKU accuracy before it enters stock.

03

Systematic Storage

Goods are slotted into optimized locations for fast, fully traceable retrieval.

04

Sorting & Cross-Docking

Orders are routed and cross-docked to cut handling time and speed dispatch.

05

Preparation & Packaging

Items are picked, branded, and packed to your specification — ready to ship.

06

Returns Management

Returned goods are inspected, restocked, or processed — closing the loop end to end.
